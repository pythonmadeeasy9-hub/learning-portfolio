Marketing that connects strategy with execution

I work at the intersection of strategy, creativity, and execution. My role spans campaign planning, content development, email marketing, social media management, performance tracking, and team collaboration.

I enjoy taking a marketing objective, breaking it into actionable initiatives, building the right content and communication strategy, executing across channels, and learning from the results.

As a senior marketing professional, I also work closely with junior team members — helping them improve their thinking, execution quality, communication, and understanding of marketing fundamentals.
